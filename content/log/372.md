I was walking with the dog early this morning. It was time to take in
the vibrant mood of the world. I am more of an eyes-and-ears person,
while the dog definitely puts his nose to good use.
We had no errands other than to walk around the neighborhood.

People were already up and about, moving and making noise. On one
street, senior citizens danced to Zumba inside a multipurpose hall. On
another street, kids in classrooms played with and yelled about new
things at school. Finally, on our street, construction workers were
grinding away at metal.
