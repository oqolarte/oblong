It is just one of those good days worth sharing.

## What makes today good

Definitely not because of Amerikkka's 250th year of so-called
independence.

No, today is good because we are graced by simple yet joyful moments:

- During our morning walk with the dog, a chihuahua without leash came
  out of nowhere. Right behind it was the human, a tall man with greying
  hair. He picked up the chihuahua, smiled at us and thanked us for our
  patience, and went away to go back home. All in all, a wholesome
  moment.
- Moments before the encounter above, I picked up scrapwood in
  relatively good condition from the street. I carried the slabs the
  entire walk with the dog.
- We picked up a stray avocado fruit that had fallen off from its
  branch. Days later, it would be delicious to have in breakfast.
- The largest banana sprout is now bearing fruit again after, ahem,
  offering several sacrificial gifts.
- As requested, I built a simple shelf for journals and notebooks. From
  scrapwood! Costing me *only* my time, labor, and patience.
