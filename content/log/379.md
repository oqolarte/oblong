## Recent food trip

The following are *NOT* Michelin-starred restaurants, but the food I
have eaten at these deserves their own constellation.

### Cosmic Kapitolyo

They serve good vegan food that mimics meat in both taste and texture,
all using plant-based proteins and perhaps panache. Their tofu sisig
shocks me. Max's must visit a Cosmic branch, order a bowl (for it is
served in a bowl), and take notes.

### Sichu Malatang

Sichuan-inspired spices flavor whatever ingredients one adds to their
stainless steel bowl. The Hot Pot is a good default---they would not
have named the place
"[malatang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malatang)" otherwise.  But I
prefer their "Dry Pot": stir-fried with soy sauce, oils, and lots of
spices.

### New Food City

Another Chinese-themed food, sweetened to meet the Filipino
palate in the middle. Affordable. Service is relatively quick.
