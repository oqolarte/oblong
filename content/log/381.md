Detecting nuances amid the noise is a challenge. There is no
one way to get better at it, except through experience. Having a life
outside of the digital space helps.

Read books to learn different human perspectives. Whether you finish a
book, it has served its purpose by adding its thoughts to
you, like how food, good or bad, contributes to your overall nutrition.

Also, dip once in a while in the din of performances in social media, if
only to get a feel of what it is like to yell at echo chambers. The
allure of wanting to be seen and heard by people cannot be ignored.
After all, if we want to share our ideas, we must meet other people
where they are at some point.

Hot takes are for edgelords and attention seekers. They often stir
negative emotions for clout, and if they had the patience to dive into
the details of their argument, it is done poorly. Nuance adds depth to
discourse, and it involves taking the time to consider and understand
"other sides".
