## Siyudad pop

We recently saw the performance of a local siyudad pop band, [ALYSON](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6p3DdkV9KXorGAUtS1pANA), play
live. Before them, though, were equally electrifying sets from
[Paprikka](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvaWlpgnQ9lbCm1JrAOjzzQ), and [Uncle Bob's Funky Seven Club](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJU3uaru5zKpW_ULDAcm3sA).
All in all, it was a night of tight performances.

Siyoudad pop---more like New Manila Sound---is a play on the city pop
genre, itself a concoction of sounds and visual aesthetics from jazz,
funk, and pop in and around the 70s and 80s. JC G. even argues that
"[Filipinos are the grandfathers of city pop](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVvz030Eoso/)" in his Instagram
Reel.  It is worth reiterating though that without the Black community
in New Orleans, jazz music itself would not have been developed.
Moreover, around the same time (70s and 80s), "Manila Sound" was a
thing, which arguably is the spiritual sibling, sound-wise, of City Pop.
