## Creative fiction

PROBLEM → METAPHOR → [PLOT](/plot)

First, think of a problem. The more personal it is, the more you know
how it feels, the more you can dissect it.

Then, think about a metaphor for it. So, for example, one might feel
like young adulthood is difficult to navigate (problem) as though wading
through the rough seas with no life-vest (metaphor).

Finally, think of a plot that fits the metaphor. Following through with
the example metaphor, it could be a story about a person surviving a
shipwreck who is now in the middle of ocean with barely anything to
survive.
