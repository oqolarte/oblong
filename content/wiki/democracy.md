## Intro

Democracy is the rule of majority.
It is still a form of [hierarchy](/hierarchy), in
which the desires of majority trample those of the minority.

Democracy is therefore rigged to appease the majority and
[make the process profitable](/capitalism) to keep the status quo.

At least within the [so-called Philippines](/pilipinas), its
implementation is a sham.  It is impossible for anyone to legislate for
everyone else. All of us have our own unique experiences, our own life
stories that cannot be considered by lawmakers, no matter their claims
of wanting to represent us.

## Democracy as a form of domination

Democracy has forced us into a mindset that belittles certain groups,
because we have already made up our minds on what 'good' decision making
is. Power to all the people means power to everyone, not those whose
opinions we value more over others.

This means that we should not enforce the will of the majority over the
minority. And that we shoud allow space for those who have different
opinions and beliefs than us, without resorting to wanting to destroy
each other.
