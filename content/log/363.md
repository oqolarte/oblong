## Brief review of *The Heart of a Dog*

An interesting and genuinely funny satire by Mikhail Bulgakov set
during Russian Revolution. It tracks a wounded stray dog and how
he---yes, *he*---is fetched from the streets by a medical doctor (world
renowned, the reader would later find out). What happens after this
seemingly random act of kindness *humanizes* a desparate dog only for
odd-funny things to happen.
