## Thoughts on *The Woman From Uruguay*

The observations made by Lucas Pereyra, the protagonist-narrator, are
funny to me because truths of other people, when inspected through a
humorous lens, have the possibility to become my truths.

I have only encountered the text in English, thanks to the translator
Jennifer Croft, who did a great job at conveying the insufferable
Lucas's day trip to the English speakers. I wonder what Croft must have
felt when reading and working on the narrator's sexual fantasies that
appears to be common in men in literary communities.

"Beach hedonism" could be my least favorite hedonism, with all its sand
getting in every fold. Beach is supposed to be the perfect location for
sexual unfoldings between people. But not all sand are powdery smooth;
not all waves are calm; not all tides will avoid swallowing you whole;
and the golden hour does not last long; so tell me of the hype again?
