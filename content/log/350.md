We intended to watch some dark comedy films and here were some that we
crossed off our list:

## The films so far

***Kind Hearts & Coronets***  
A series of criminal acts narrated by the perpetrator himself on the night
before his execution, the protagonists lays it all, because the truth is
all that matters now when death awaits in the morning, and not, say, his
supposedly rightful inheritance.

***A New Leaf***  
Rich men are stupid in their own big and small ways. Imagine blowing
through your entire inheritance and having no back-up plans. And the
only solution you come up with is to marry [back] into wealth? It makes
for a funny film---funny in its own big and small ways.

***Dr. Strangelove***  
...is a Cold War satire. It is an okay film. The only female character,
though, did not even pass the Bechdel test, which was nigh impossible
given that this is a Kubrick film.
