I did not know that being nerd about one thing---for example,
paper---and making it your only purpose in life would have you willing
to do *anything* to secure a fulfilling career revolving around it.

This film by Park Chan-wook also reminded me of a similar-different
fiction: the anime series [*My Home Hero*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Home_Hero). *No Other Choice*
and *My Home Hero* are similar in portraying how fatherhead will go
great lengths to "provide" and/or "protect" their family. However, the
former feels more sinister because of how the goals were achieved.

The main character in *No Other Choice*, when his life, and consequently
his family's, is falling apart, eliminates his competition, and without
explicitly telling anyone about it, justifies his actions, simply with
how he faces the results of his actions.

How many men have justified their drastic actions with their own
personal experience of suffering? Dominant systems that comprise the
[empire](/empire) do not break this cycle, only reward those who turn
back on their ethics and principles to willingly participate in the
empire's feedback loops, at the expense of... everything else, leading
to [destruction](/destruction) of lives.
