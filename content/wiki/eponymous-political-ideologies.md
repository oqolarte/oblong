## Preamble

Why do some leftist philosophies and politics (e.g., Marxism, Leninism,
Maoism, etc.) are named like that?

By making their politics eponymous, their believers have already put
them on a pedestal.

> imo any -ism that starts with the name of a person is incompatible
> with [prefigurative politics](/prefiguration) because i want to walk
> the talk of living people, not dead ones.
>  
> -- [pepe](https://social.lol/@pepe/117455602889699819)

## Roadblocks

Eponyms in this context, in my opinion, can be blocks. Namedropping
thinkers and also speaking in academic terms prevent the laypeople from
resonating with progressive and radical politics that might lead them to
[do something meaningful](/direct-action) with their present
conditions.

A better way of [communicating](/communication) might be to distill the
main ideas to their core and find a way to present them that is relevant
to the context of their everyday life.

And anyway, I resist using big-name -isms---of the Left,
especially---in that their respective thinkers would not have added
something valuable had they not stood on the shoulders of giants that
came before them. And by giants, I mean to include entire egalitarian
communities and Indigenous Peoples who have long thought and lived
radically way before armchair philosophers theorized them to paper.
