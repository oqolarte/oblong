- Finished another [book](/books) (graphic novel).

## Thoughts on *Ghost World*

One day after shuffling around books in our shelves, I saw that we have
this graphic novel by Daniel Clowes. I did not know that *Ghost World*
was a graphic novel first before it was adapted into a film, starring
Scarlett Johansson and Thora Birch. The film I watched some ten-plus
years ago when I was around the same age as the main characters.

Back then, i.e., when I watched the film, I resonated with the
characters' aimlessness attached to a particular kind of youth in a
particular phase in their life. After reading the graphic novel now, not
so much, obviously.

The setting and the characters depicted in them cannot be any more
different than my lived experience, but I still found some dialogues
funny and absurd.

## Third wisdom tooth extracted

The operation took about an hour. The dentist, I feel, found my
case---an impacted tooth---to work on. Counting the waiting time prior
to that, I was on that public hospital for two hours more or less.

### Pre-op: Waiting time

Time is the price you pay for wanting to get---and succeeding at it
after patiently wading through state's bureaucracy---the promised
affordable healthcare insurance.

During the wait in the holding area, I pulled out a book and began to
read. It was hard to conenctrate on the text, because some of the people
in there with me were doomscrolling on their phones in *loudspeaker*.

Hearing bits of the "reels", it got me thinking: regular exposure to big
tech's social media algorithms normalize, statistically speaking, the
collective consciousness of those who frequent those platforms. For
example, at the time of writing, many people are hooked on AI-generated
Chinese dramas, of which I have only seen snippets.

Ethics, unable to catch up with tech's rapid development, seem to
conform around the use of the emerging tools, both hardware and
software. Humane design that could have addressed accessibility and
ethical concenrs is, unfortunately, deferred to future to make way for
profitability.

Book in hand, I sit on the periphery, on the outside looking in. And the
allure of the algorithm---even the idea of *curating* it to suit one's
taste---shimmers still. It is impossible not to be tempted to give in.

### Post-op

I can still feel and hear the grining of the dentist's tools in my
skull. The unnerving sensation is left imprinted on memory. I hope it
goes away soon.
