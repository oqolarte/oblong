This week's update from me.

## MIBF 2026

We cancelled going to the Manila International Book Fair 2026 at the
literal (figurative) last minute, which I think was for the better, for
a few reasons:

- We should not really hoard books;
- We have so much to read in our library; and
- Based on the social media feeds, the fair was jam-packed from first to
  last day.

## Job prospects?

One afternoon, three different headhunters called me for three different
job prospects. I was only expecting a call from one of them.

It was weird because they all called within an hour.

I immediately turned down the last one because the "offer" was full-
time on-site, and the site was in a central business district several
cities away.

I also felt cool when, with one of them, I mentioned my being
"old-school" and that I prefer to be contacted via SMS and/or email.

## Pusoy Dos miracle?

To pass the time, we played [Pusoy Dos](https://www.wikihow.com/Pusoy-Dos)---sometimes dubbed "Filipino Poker"---with friends.

We were all having a fine time mildly trash-talking each other when, in
one of the rounds, I could not believe what I saw in my hands.

{{< figure src="/image/pusoy-dos.jpg" alt="two sets of five-combination cards are laid on a table. on the left hand is a royal flush consisting of 10, Jack, Queen, King, and Ace of Clubs. on the right hand is a straight flush consisting of cards 3 to 7 of Clubs." caption="Royal flush vs straight flush in the same game with cards I personally shuffled and dealt" >}}

A straight flush!

I even looked it up to check if indeed it was a straight flush before
putting down the banger, to our collective awe.

Even more awesome, though, was when another player flashbanged us with
their even higher set of cards---a royal flush!

I took the picture above to commemorate this once-in-a-lifetime (?)
event in my pusoy dos gaming.
