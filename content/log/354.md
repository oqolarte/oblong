We should allow ourselves to glance at our past and study it, if only
for a while, how we used to glorify certain events and symbols of
futuristic hope. But let us not forget the rose-tinted glasses we were
wearing then, or the ignorance that did not reveal what are now
obvious flaws.

The world, and everyone in it, has changed since.

Revisiting nostalgia must only be a tool with which to look at and into
ourselves. I realize this after watching *Only Yesterday* (1991) from
Studio Ghibli, a wholesome film about remembering a specific
version of the self.
