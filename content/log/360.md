This film by Wim Wenders follows the life of middle-aged Hirayama, a
janitor cleaning Tokyo's public toilets, going through his routine. His
is a [simple life](/simple-living) with simpler joys. But even
simplicity must sometimes be punctuated by unforeseen events, the
unfolding of which are mostly beyond our control.

Living alone sustained by a few creative hobbies I would have found
ideal years ago---perhaps write about the mundane in purple prose; shoot
a film about the mundane; upload the entire production of the entire
mundane in the internet as a video and call it low budget film. But now,
I would rather spend days living with and/or within walking distance of
people I love.

As shown in the film, simple living need not lead to austerity.
Hirayama, as part of his routine, also indulges in creative pursuits
like film photography, analog muzak (cassette tapes), and books.
