## What is tropical internet

From the link above (hyperlink/s added by me):

> We term the 'tropical internet' to speak to condition of the online as
> experienced/lived from the  tropic regions of the
> [Philippines](/pilipinas) and broader Southeast Asia, concerned with
> heat, extremes, monsoons, distance, islandedness, improvisation,
> pluralities, and diasporas.  Think extremes and edges: how
> [technology](/technology) operates under those conditions as a
> totalizing infrastructure. We view the [internet](/internet) not as a
> frictionless, invisible global system but as an
> [ecology](/social-ecology): a living, intertwined layer to the world,
> one that is vulnerable, at risk, adaptive, entangled, and continuously
> rewritten by those who live at the eye of the storm and its
> peripheries. We think of fragility, ecology, maintenance, and
> vernacular ingenuities that sustain connection in the global south.
