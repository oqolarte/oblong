We saw and heard a busker inside Gateway Mall (Cubao, QC), and his
mash-up of U2's "I still haven't found what I'm looking for" and Donna
Lewis's "I love you always forever" was impeccable and tasteful. His
voice was unique enough that it stood out from the crowd's din.

He also covered Gotye's "Somebody that I used to know", which by the
time I heard him playing it, he was slightly muffled as we had already
entered a bookshop to browse some books. As my brain has always homed in
on muzak signals, I could not really focus on the book titles in front
of me.

We left the busker a Php 70 tip.

Our sin that we must carry from here on out is that we did not know his
name nor how to contact or follow him on the internet.
