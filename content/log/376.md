*Schrödinger's incel: white man simultaneously takes and escapes
accountability by his own medicine*

What if a softer misogyny exists---one given power over a single life?
I imagine it would be a kind of disdain for women that is invisible.
Bear (played by Michael Johnston) carries it quietly. It is so well
hidden that none of his friends know about it.

Perhaps, this misogyny is in the form of unrequited "love" that would
morph into an obsession when left unprocessed. Investing feelings in
someone does not necessarily mean a person loves them. Bear *thinks* he
loves Nikki (played by Inde Navarrette), but by failing to be honest and
express his feelings for her, he retreats into a delusion that there
exists a reality where they can be happy together in a romantic
relationship.

When the delusion becomes real, Bear reveals his truer nature---he is
selfish right up until the end.

*Obsession*, to me, is what happens when the male leads' bigotry in
rom-coms like *Friends* and *How I Met Your Mother* is no longer funny,
but becomes a hard pill to swallow.
