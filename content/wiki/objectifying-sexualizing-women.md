## "Women are not objects."

Yes, I agree. No one should objectify women.

Some women, though, objectify themselves and/or explicitly allow others
to objectify them, as a kink. It is done with consent from all parties
involved, and is followed by some form of aftercare (emotional/physical
care after intense experiences). These kind of women are a minority.
Objectifying the self as a woman, whatever her reasons, is a privilege
that not all women could do or want to do.

**Women should have complete autonomy over their own bodies.**

## "People who sexualize women have no right to be called human beings..."

I could only imagine the emotions that would spur a woman to write such
a generalization. Women have to endure daily hardships brought by
patriarchy. I know how machismo corrodes women’s welfare.

Although a minority, even women and other
non-men can be misogynistic. [Patriarchy](/patriarchy) affects
everyone's behavior, regardless of sex and gender. This [open letter by Yannah Angela](https://yannahscreativenonfiction.substack.com/p/on-patriarchy-queerness-and-sexual)
comes to mind as an example, in which the author writes about (trigger
warning) sexual assault and child abuse as a direct consequence of
patriarchy.

Also, are women who sexualize---maybe even objectify---themselves for
whatever reason included in this reduction?

Now, it is normal to be physically attracted to, to sexualize even, the
opposite sex (women) and other feminine-presenting people (if you get
what I mean). It is the following actions *and* inactions that matter
more. Any actions that lead to, or any inactions that neither prevent
nor stop, [violence](/violence) against women ([online](/online-vaw) or
in the physical world), including sexual harassment, assault, and abuse,
should be condemned and be held accountable.

Some examples:
- Masturbating to the *thought* of some person one has sexualized in
  their heads is okay, but masturbating to revenge porn is detestable.
- Getting turned on by the *idea* of having sex with some stranger on
  the internet is okay, but sliding in their DMs unprompted to let them
  know of the fact is creepy.
- Consensual freaky and nasty sex is great, but if the safeword (a
  pre-agreed word or phrase to stop) is
  uttered, stop immediately and proceed to aftercare.
