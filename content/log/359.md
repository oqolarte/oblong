*Mag-iina* was a 2026 Cinemalaya film festival entry.[^cinemalaya]

Pegging the film as horror, I think, only served to mentally prepare its
viewers for the absurd things that would unfold. Surely, the scenes
*are* horrifying in ways where things that deal with generational trauma
are horrifying. If you, the viewer, are like me, then you will move your
head slightly back as if to distance yourself from the dread, which is
portrayed as merely the entrance to abuse from which feels so hard to
escape.

I also think the whole film is a metaphor for all suffering the
[so-called Philippines](/pilipinas) has been going through.

[^cinemalaya]: Unfortunately, because the rain has been pouring hard all
over Metro Manila, we were unable to watch the other entries.
