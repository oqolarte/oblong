Three years ago, on the early days of New Year then, we found Yogini
Melbourne's [30-day yoga challenge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eiZc7iQ8Cs0&list=PLE39V1yqWM_H6rR6Rwj6PObvAmqDTeRu5).
Life happened, and we manage to only get to, I think, day 5 or 6.

We are restarting with the challenge this month. I am happy to report
that, as of writing, we are on our 8th day streak.

In all of the sessions so far, Paula, the yogi, prompts the viewer to
recite to self their "[sankalpa](https://www.arhantayoga.org/blog/what-is-sankalpa-meaning/)".

Every day thus far, my sankalpa is:

> I am exactly where I need to be.

I also recite *my* Tagalog translation to myself:

> Naririto ako kung saan ako nararapat.
