## Brief review of *Heretics of Dune*

The story picks up some 1,500 years after the ending of the
previous book, [*God Emperor of Dune*](/348/#thoughts-on-god-emperor-of-dune).
It got weirdly horny towards the end without it turning into smut, if
that makes sense. Frank Herbert---we should know by now after reading
this Book 5 of the Dune Saga---really has a breeding kink.

On a more serious note, it is also, for me, both a long stroll and a
dizzying series of jumps through "folding space" between different sets
of characters. Still, I commend Herbert for writing the saga in tight
prose.
