Today, we made two trips to PAWS Animal Rehabilitation Center along
Katipunan Avenue. The first one was in the morning when we brought the
neighborhood cat Benny G, because we scheduled her [to be spayed](https://paws.org.ph/spay-neuter/) that day; and the second in
the afternoon after said operation.

The attendant revealed that Benny, unknown to anyone until then, had
been suffering from [pyometra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyometra),
a potentially life-threating infection of the uterus that causes it to
fill with bacteria and pus. Included during the Benny's spaying was the
removal of the pyometric uterus.

As of writing, Benny G is on her way to recovery.
