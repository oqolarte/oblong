## My last meal

Unsurprisingly, it skews Asian, mostly Filipino, foods.

### First course

- Mapo tofu
- Pancit palabok (bihon)

### Second course

- Sinigang na salmon, with calamansi as the souring agent
- White rice

### Third course

- Halo-halo
- Black coffee (barako)
