## What it is?

Prefiguration happens when there is an alignment of means and ends.

When we embody the [revolution](/revolution) as daily practice, we are
also forging a clearer path for those who would like to join us towards
world*s* unlike this one.

A life worth living in, and not escaping from, can only be possible if
enough of us agree that it is possible now, and actually work on making
it happen.

## On imagining alternatives

Margaret Thatcher's mantra was "There is no alternative." This is a
lazy, if not dangerous, rhetoric to be spouting.

French anthropologist [Pierre Clastres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Clastres)
suggested that simple and more
innocent societies without rulers are more imaginative than their ruled
counterparts.

As humans, it is much more fulfilling to think of dozens of
alternatives, from the most mundane to the most hip zeitgeist.

## Why prefigurative politics

1. We do it because we want to have something concrete, whether it
   ultimately fails, to which we can
   point when someone asks, "Is there an example of *this* ever
   working?" Now, some examples of spaces and communities that made
   radical changes to their conditions:
    - [FaSinPat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FaSinPat)/Zanon in
      Argentina
    - [Rojava Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rojava_Revolution); [The Rojava Revolution in Peril](https://crimethinc.com/2026/02/09/the-rojava-revolution-in-peril-the-struggle-for-free-life-continues-a-statement-from-american-chinese-and-russian-internationalists); [The Reflections of a Russiahn Anarchist in Rojava](https://crimethinc.com/2024/12/19/news-from-the-front-the-reflections-of-a-russian-anarchist-in-rojava)
    - [Zapatistas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapatista_Army_of_National_Liberation) in Mexico
    - [Black Panther Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther_Party) in USA
2. By putting radical theories into practice, we can refine the theory
   further, which in turn will improve our practice, and so on. This
   feedback loop becomes important in shaping both the theory and
   practice given our specific contexts and lived experiences.
3. Transforming our current lives into something that we want to be in
   is in itself a [joyful act](/joyful-militancy) of changing
   everything.

## Potential pitfalls

1. It could become **lifestyle activism**, that is, doing it is limited
   to the self or to a small group of people. The idea of prefiguration
   is, of course, to involve everyone else.
2. It could become **rigid radicalism**, that is, one resorts to extreme
   policing of others' behaviors when not conforming to or participating
   in the "proper way" of prefiguration. To remind us, there is no one
   way of changing everything, and we should be open and flexible to
   different ideas.
3. Adopting prefigurative politics makes one a **clear target** of the
   Empire. Detecting dissent, the empire often seeks to eliminate those
   that seek to subvert the status quo.
