I once read a rant from a woman, and it went (verbatim):

> Women are not objects. People who sexualize women have no right to be
> called human beings...

I am writing this to try to unpack this based on what I know.

{{< toc >}}

## "Women are not objects."

Yes, I agree. No one should objectify women.

Perhaps a small exception to this is when certain women "self-objectify"
and/or explicitly allow others (likely someone they trust) to objectify
them maybe as part of a kink or lifestyle; and even then, I would argue,
they are a minority. Their choices for doing so are borne out of
political privilege that very few people could do. Their body, their
choice.

## "People who sexualize women have no right to be called human beings..."

Broad, sweeping generalizations like this are understandable, especially
when it feels cathartic for the woman ranting about it who likely has to
endure daily hardships brought to her by the highly patriarchal society
we all live and participate in. Although a minority, it is worth noting
that even women and other non-men can be misogynistic, because
[patriarchy](/patriarchy) is totalizing in that it affects everyone's
behavior, no matter the sex and gender.

Now, I---as a cis-hetero male, apparently non-human being---wish to
argue for whatever is left of my personhood by saying: it is normal to
be physically attracted to, to sexualize even, the opposite sex and
other femme-presenting folks (if you get what I am saying), but it is
the following actions *and* inactions that matter more in my opinion.

Any actions that lead to, or any inactions that do not prevent or stop,
[violence](/violence) against women ([online](/online-vaw) or in meatspace),
including sexual harm, harassment, and abuse, should be condemned in any
reality.
