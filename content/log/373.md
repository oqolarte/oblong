I desire to be free from over-reliance on the [dominant systems](/empire) that
continue to wreak havoc on all life forms. And yet I know I will be left
disfigured---figuratively and/or literally---if I do so abruptly and
without proper preparation. It does not help that I am impatient.
Paradoxically, I am also sluggish.
The [projects I want to do](/di-why)
to improve our independence are, unfortunately, severely handicapped by
my lack of motivation. Sometimes, there will be bursts of inspiration.
But I must not rely on such once-in-a-while events.

Too much wallowing, too much passivity---both done in relative comfort
in this old house with this tired mind.

Comfort, I should know by now, is an easy death sentence, without death
itself arriving.

Then the other day, I had a burst of energy. Before committing to the
house chores of the day, I announced, "The [revolution](/revolution) is
calling me. It consists of invisible work" [meaning the chores].  I said
that in jest, but the longer it lingered in the air, the more joyful I
got. I did all the chores for all the vertebrates living under this roof
(myself included). It was fulfilling, even though I was spent afterward.
I cannot say I wallowed. I did things with real positive effects, a
reminder that the revolution we want to happen is in the daily errands
that keep us alive.
